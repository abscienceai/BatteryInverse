# Expanded Deconfounded Transfer Analysis

## Purpose

This analysis tests whether apparent leave-one-dataset-out performance reflects transferable cell-level degradation information or dataset-level target priors. Raw retention is compared with dataset-centered and within-dataset percentile targets.

## Interpretation

- Positive improvement means the model beats the train-median baseline.
- If a feature family performs well on raw retention but fails on centered or percentile targets, it likely exploits dataset-level priors rather than transferable within-dataset degradation residuals.
- Metadata-only performance is especially important: raw success combined with centered-target failure indicates protocol/dataset fingerprinting.
- UL_PUR has only 2 held-out cells and should be interpreted cautiously.
