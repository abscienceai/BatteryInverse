## Interpretation guide

- Large gaps between random-looking performance and leave-one-dataset-out performance indicate domain shift.
- Metadata-only success can indicate dataset/protocol priors rather than transferable degradation physics.
- Normalized capacity features test whether capacity-scale confounding is reduced.
- Transfer-Gap Index combines feature-space distance and observed error; high values indicate risky extrapolation.
- Very small held-out datasets, especially UL_PUR, should be interpreted cautiously.
